## 5. Financial Risk Analysis & Sensitivity Scenarios  

| Risk Factor | Impact if Adverse | Sensitivity Test (±10 %) | Mitigation Lever |
|-------------|-------------------|--------------------------|------------------|
| **Raw Milk Price** (baseline $2.80/gal) | +$0.30/gal raises COGS → margin ↓ 5 % | +10 % → margin falls to 50 % (EBITDA ≈ $73k) | Long‑term forward contracts with farms; diversify supplier base |
| **Subscriber Acquisition** (target 150 hh) | Shortfall to 100 hh cuts revenue 33 % | -33 % revenue → EBITDA ≈ $30k, break‑even delayed to month 12 | Incentivized referral program; partner with corporate wellness for bulk sign‑ups |
| **Vehicle Downtime** (2 weeks lost) | Lost mobile vending sales (~$1,100) | -100 % vending sales → EBITDA down $1.5k (negligible) | Preventive maintenance; backup refrigerated unit lease |
| **Regulatory Delay** (organic certification) | Postpone launch 1 mo | +1 mo cash burn $12.5k, delay revenue start | Early pre‑application, allocate contingency $5k |
| **Marketing Cost Overrun** (budget $6k) | +$4k extra spend | +66 % marketing → EBITDA down $4k | Use performance‑based digital ads; track CAC tightly |

**Overall Sensitivity** – Even with a 10 % rise in raw milk cost and a 25 % drop in subscriber count, the business stays cash‑positive by month 12 (EBITDA ≈ $30k). The primary “break‑even risk” is subscriber acquisition; therefore CAC must stay ≤ $30 per household (150 hh × $30 = $4,500 total acquisition spend).
