## 3️⃣ Return on Investment (ROI) & Break‑Even Analysis  

| **Metric** | **Year 1** | **Year 2** (30 % revenue growth) | **Year 3** (25 % growth YoY) |
|------------|-----------|----------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Revenue | $445,000 | $578,500 | $723,125 |
| Gross Profit (56 % avg.) | $248,150 | $323,560 | $404,150 |
| Operating Expenses* | $448,000 | $453,000 | $458,000 |
| **Net Income** | **‑ $3,850** | **‑ $129,440** | **‑ $53,850** |
| Cumulative Net Cash (incl. initial $300 k) | $296,150 | $166,710 | $112,860 |
| **Cumulative ROI** |  -1 % |  -44 % |  -62 % (still negative) |
| **Break‑Even (cumulative cash = 0)** | **Month 10** (≈ $310 k gross profit needed) | — | — |

\*Operating expenses = Payroll $398k + Marketing $30k + Utilities/Insurance $10k + SaaS & misc $10k.  

**Why break‑even occurs in month 10**  

- Gross profit needed to cover *cash outflow* (CapEx $300k + 6‑mo runway $150k) ≈ $310k.  
- Projected monthly gross profit (based on ramp‑up) reaches $35k by month 9 → cumulative crosses $310k in month 10.  

**ROI Outlook (3‑yr)**  

- Assuming margin improvement (negotiated lower fabric cost, higher subscription retention) and modest expense discipline, a **conservative upside scenario** yields cumulative net profit of **+$120k** by end of Year 3 → **ROI ≈ 40 %** on the original $300k equity.
